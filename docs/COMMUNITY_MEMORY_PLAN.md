## Evidence boundaries

The page may explain documented aims without presenting them as deployed
capabilities. Illustrative orchard records must be explicitly fictional.
Changing example sliders does not stake a real thought or train a model.
Local inference and a privacy tier are different concepts. Current stock-model
integration is not automatically the contextual Trust architecture.

## Restoration

at their original relative paths. To restore one card, take its section HTML,
relevant JavaScript renderer/listeners, and CSS from that directory. Keep its
proposal status. Do not load archived scripts into the current entry point.
