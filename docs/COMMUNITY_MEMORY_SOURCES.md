# Sources for the community memory edition

This edition explains the documented local Cognicist learning process. The
orchard participants, dates, numerical assessments and outcomes are fictional.
They are an educational example, not evidence of a running community or model.

Primary texts were read in the sibling `iris/byjohnash/cognicist-literature/`
repository. Filenames below identify that local source corpus. These notes
separate the ideas in the writing from implementation claims.

## Principles and privacy

**The Cognicist Manifesto.txt**: Preamble; Confluence and Privacy; Temporal
Focus; Collective Feedback; Server Types; Self-Governance.

The preamble establishes autonomy of minds. Confluence and Privacy distinguishes
personal, local and global thoughts and explicitly places personal thoughts on
the user's local device. Server Types distinguishes raw local records from the
global representational layer. Collective Feedback gives people a continuing
role in responding to a community model's synthesis. The page does not invent a
new cryptographic protocol to explain these commitments.

**Rhizome.md**: FourThought Is the Shared Language; Rhizome Is the Process Around
the Language; A Network Without a Central Ledger; Memory Under Human Control.

The local workflow is investigation, distillation, review, staking, relating,
selective sharing, exploration and learning over time. Model-produced drafts
are reviewed; reading another person's contribution does not transfer its
authorship. The original author and the later interpreter remain distinguishable.

Current application behavior is documented in the sibling Rhizome repository:
`docs/fourthought-local-ledger.md` and `docs/rhizome-network.md`. Private input to
suggest/distill is restricted to authorized local inference; it is not sent to
API/subscription inference. The page's privacy cards explain intended sharing
scopes, not a deployed per-record/per-peer permission editor or immunity from a
compromised host.

## Staking and the two axes

**Teaching Iris.md**: FourThought as a Staking Dialectic; The Rhizome Feedback
Loop; FourThought as RLHF.

The four forms are reflections, statements, predictions and questions. Verity
records perceived truth; valence records the value or desirability attached to
meaning or outcomes. People can correct a model's interpretation before staking.
Questions open inquiry rather than asserting an answer. The demonstration
therefore introduces the axes through claims rather than treating a question
as a proposition whose truth must be rated.

The controls only change browser memory. They do not submit a stake, infer a
person's actual values, update a model, or connect to a live ledger.

## The orchard and community memory

**FourThought as a Participatory Design Process.txt**: the orchard scenario and
its mapping of tasks, resources, claims and evolving understanding.

**belief_staking.txt**: Information That Ages Well; From Prediction to Proof of
Judgment.

**Time Compass - Navigating Time with Iris.md**: temporal motivations and the
FourThought dialectic.

The orchard demonstrates a question, a prediction, a second person's assessment
of the same claim, and a later reflection. Its restatement preserves the claim
text while changing the speaker's assessment. This parallels the documented
local vote/restake semantics without suggesting that majority votes settle
truth. No averaged community score is fabricated. Moving the history cutoff
hides later contributions from the view; it does not modify earlier records.

The reflection deliberately leaves causality open: fewer lost fruits coincide
with both weekly harvests and more volunteers. The example does not turn a
reported outcome into proof of an intervention's effect.

## Learning through the history

**Teaching Iris.md**: Individual Irises; The Prophet Incentive; Social Proof of
Impact; Fruitful Questioning; Reflective Compression.

**social_proof_of_impact.md** and **Rhizome.md** distinguish foresight from action
that helps produce a wanted outcome. The page presents these as documented
learning directions, not automatic point awards, a fixed ranking of people, or
training implemented by this static site.

The corpus includes actual low-dimensional Iris/Trust learning work. This
edition neither substitutes the browser example for those experiments nor
claims that ordinary stock-model use in Rhizome automatically implements that
architecture. The presentation's focus is the participatory history through
which learning can occur.

## Many communities and the larger vision

**The Purple Pill Manifesto.txt**: Confluence; Irises; Reflexivity; Localism.
The public link is the canonical URL embedded in the locally generated article:
https://speakerjohnash.com/literature/the-purple-pill-manifesto.html
It explains Irises as shared voices and lenses through which communities can
reflect on their own beliefs and explore other perspectives. Older privacy
formulations in that text are read alongside current authorized-local-inference
intent, rather than replacing it.

**Teaching Iris.md**: Individual Irises.

**beyond_the_ledger.md**: The Planetary Starter; Two Complementary Forms of
Distillation; FourThought Is the Shared Semantic Interface; Local Sovereignty
Is Exploration; Compression Is Synchronization.

Different communities can teach different Irises. The shared substrate can
carry symbolic knowledge and learned representations; new communities inherit
a starting point and remain free to diverge. The page's description of
open-source model weights from differently fine-tuned Irises follows John's
explicit direction for this edition. It does not equate the larger FLC with
peer transport, a raw-text database, or a cryptographic protocol.

## Editorial boundary
