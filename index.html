08 / TEACHING IRISLEARNING THROUGH PARTICIPATION

THE FEEDBACK MOVES BOTH WAYS

A community teaches.
An Iris learns its world.

An Iris learns through the relationships between sources, thoughts, values, and outcomes. People can inspect and correct how their thoughts are interpreted. Later responses add to the history from which learning can take place.

WHAT RHIZOME IS FOR

A tool for building the larger vision.

Rhizome’s purpose is to bootstrap belief staking in community. It gives people a way to simulate and practice these processes through a collaborative learning dialectic and a peer-to-peer underground knowledge commons.

People can put what matters to them on record, compare perspectives, and co-learn toward shared goals — including building the larger vision itself. Rhizome is designed to plug into future systems such as the FLC, Iris, and Ŧrust. It is a tool for developing that vision together, rather than a claim that the vision is already complete.

IRIS · A MEDIATOR FOR COMMUNITY UNDERSTANDING

An Iris is a language model designed to act as a mediator of community understanding. It brings people’s different beliefs into conversation, reflects their perspectives back to them, and helps articulate where they agree, disagree, or need to investigate further.

People can question and correct that synthesis. This continuing exchange between individual voices and a shared voice is confluence, described in the Purple Pill Manifesto ↗. Today, Rhizome’s local and API models function as proto-Irises in this mediating role.

A COMMUNAL OODA LOOP

Orient toward truth and goodness, together.

Observe, orient, decide, act — then return to what happened. Iris mediates the conversation; the community compares perspectives, chooses what to do, and revisits the consequences. Shared memory connects what people expected with what they did and what followed.

01 / OBSERVE

Bring experience.

Share observations, questions, and evidence with their sources and times.

02 / ORIENT

Make sense together.

Use Iris to surface different explanations. Distinguish what seems true from what people value.

03 / DECIDE

Choose a direction.

Stake expectations and consider actions that could bring about a future the community wants.

04 / ACT & RETURN

Learn from consequences.

Act, observe outcomes, and reflect. Feed what changed back into the next round of inquiry.

Making sense of causality takes repeated inquiry: what changed, what did we do, and what else could explain the outcome? An accurate prediction and an action that helped produce a good outcome are different contributions. The Prophet Incentive and Social Proof of Impact connect these two parts of the loop.

AN OPEN HISTORY FOR LEARNING

More than one way to learn.

The stake history is designed to let other scoring and learning functions operate over it too. Communities can bring different objectives to the same attributed record of claims, questions, judgments, and outcomes.

These are documented directions for teaching Iris. A demonstration on this page does not update model weights. Rhizome's record and review process supplies the history; the learning architecture uses that history.

10 / PRIVATE, LOCAL, GLOBALCHOOSING WHAT WE SHARE

PERSONAL MEMORY, SHARED LEARNING

Choose what
enters the conversation.

FourThought distinguishes private reflection, local participation, and contributions intended for wider sharing. Local computing has been part of the privacy intention from the beginning.

●

Private

Personal reflection remains yours. A chosen, authorized local model can help you reason with it without sending it to a remote model.

● ●

Local

Contribute to the people and communities with whom you choose to share. Local knowledge can retain its language, values, and context.

● ● ●

Global

Intentionally contribute beyond the local community. The larger vision carries learning outward without requiring everyone's private history to become public.

11 / MANY COMMUNITIES, MANY IRISESCONNECTED TO A LARGER VISION

LOCAL KNOWLEDGE CAN KEEP GROWING

Different histories.
Different worldviews.

A neighborhood, a research group, and an archive may ask different questions and cultivate different Irises. FourThought gives them a shared language while leaving room for their differences.

In the Purple Pill Manifesto ↗, an Iris is a way for many voices to form a shared voice, reflect their beliefs back to them, and explore other worldviews. That continuing exchange is called confluence.

The local models and API models used with Rhizome function as proto-Irises: they help communities investigate, interpret, and reflect on their shared memory. Community-specific fine-tuned weights belong to the larger learning vision.

THE LARGER LEARNING VISION

What communities learn can help others begin.

Rhizome is designed to connect into a larger vision involving open-source model weights from many differently fine-tuned Irises, each representing a community worldview. Shared learning can give future Irises a starting point from which their communities continue to learn and diverge.

The local ledger is part of that process. The larger federated learning layer concerns learned representations and model parameters as well as shared thoughts.

12 / FEDERATED LEARNING CHAINA COMMONS FOR MANY POSSIBLE FUTURES

SHARE A STARTING POINT. KEEP ROOM TO DIVERGE.

A global store.
Many local directions.

The Federated Learning Chain is envisioned as a global store that compresses knowledge deemed useful across peoples into tensor form. Local Irises encode their communities’ local knowledge; the shared starter carries learning intended to benefit people more broadly. Irises stake to this system as representatives of their communities, with the provenance of each thought traced back to the community members who contributed it.
