01 / THE FOUNDATIONAUTONOMY & COLLECTIVE LEARNING

ROOM FOR EACH MIND

Think for yourself.
Learn with others.

Cognicism begins with the autonomy of minds and the possibility of learning together. Different experiences and perspectives give a community more to learn from.

01

Belong without surrendering.

Personal reflection can remain private while we participate in a community.

02

Connect by mutual choice.

Independent communities choose how they participate and what they share.

03

Let different voices remain.

Disagreement belongs in the record. A shared understanding can keep evolving.

04

Remember without freezing.

Interpretation can change while the earlier thought remains available to revisit.

From The Cognicist Manifesto and Rhizome.

03 / COMMUNITY MEMORYA HISTORY WE CAN REVISIT

MORE THAN THE LATEST OPINION

Our minds change.
The conversation continues.

A later reflection can refer back to an earlier prediction. Another person can disagree. Their different judgments remain attached to their voices, giving the community a history of how its understanding developed.

04 / TEACHING IRISLEARNING THROUGH PARTICIPATION

THE FEEDBACK MOVES BOTH WAYS

A community teaches.
An Iris learns its world.

The literature describes an Iris learning through the relationships between sources, thoughts, values, and outcomes. People can inspect and correct how their thoughts are interpreted. Later responses add to the history from which learning can take place.

01

Investigate

Read, listen, ask questions, and gather evidence.

02

Review & stake

Put what you mean on record, with your own judgments.

03

Respond & act

Add another perspective, test an expectation, or take action.

04

Reflect & learn

Revisit what happened and carry the learning into the next inquiry.

WHAT CAN THE HISTORY TEACH?Different contributions matter in different ways
THE PROPHET INCENTIVE

Who saw something before it became clear?

Preserving the original claim and its surrounding disagreement lets later understanding recognize contextual foresight, including insights that were initially unpopular.

ŦRUST · CONTEXTUAL ATTENTION

Who matters for this question, at this time?

Technically, Ŧrust brings source, time, and content into the attention mechanism. It learns which contributions to attend to in context, rather than assigning a person one permanent score.

AN OPEN HISTORY FOR LEARNING

More than one way to learn.

The stake history is designed to let other scoring and learning functions operate over it too. Communities can bring different objectives to the same attributed record of claims, questions, judgments, and outcomes.

These are documented directions for teaching Iris. A demonstration on this page does not update model weights. Rhizome's record and review process supplies the history; the learning architecture uses that history.

From Teaching Iris, Social Proof of Impact, and Rhizome.

05 / PRIVATE, LOCAL, GLOBALCHOOSING WHAT WE SHARE

PERSONAL MEMORY, SHARED LEARNING

Choose what
enters the conversation.

FourThought distinguishes private reflection, local participation, and contributions intended for wider sharing. Local computing has been part of the privacy intention from the beginning.

●

Private

Personal reflection remains yours. A chosen, authorized local model can help you reason with it without sending it to a remote model.

● ●

Local

Contribute to the people and communities with whom you choose to share. Local knowledge can retain its language, values, and context.

● ● ●

Global

Intentionally contribute beyond the local community. The larger vision carries learning outward without requiring everyone's private history to become public.

From the manifesto's Confluence and Privacy and Beyond the Ledger.

06 / MANY COMMUNITIES, MANY IRISESCONNECTED TO A LARGER VISION

LOCAL KNOWLEDGE CAN KEEP GROWING

Different histories.
Different worldviews.

A neighborhood, a research group, and an archive may ask different questions and cultivate different Irises. FourThought gives them a shared language while leaving room for their differences.

In the Purple Pill Manifesto ↗, an Iris is a way for many voices to form a shared voice, reflect their beliefs back to them, and explore other worldviews. That continuing exchange is called confluence.

THE LARGER LEARNING VISION

What communities learn can help others begin.

Rhizome is designed to connect into a larger vision involving open-source model weights from many differently fine-tuned Irises, each representing a community worldview. Shared learning can give future Irises a starting point from which their communities continue to learn and diverge.

The local ledger is part of that process. The larger federated learning layer concerns learned representations and model parameters as well as shared thoughts.

From Teaching Iris and Beyond the Ledger. This describes the larger vision, not a model-sharing service running in this page.
