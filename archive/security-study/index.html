01 / THE CONTRACTFREEDOM WITHOUT EXPOSURE

THE FIRST DESIGN DECISION

A connected world
without a master key.

Open-source code. Human-centered admission. Independent communities. Strong protection for each person's information. These ideas strengthen one another rather than compete.

01

Belong without surrendering.

Joining a community never opens a personal vault.

02

Connect by mutual choice.

A trusted neighbor cannot admit strangers on your behalf.

03

Reason without revealing.

An answer may move where its underlying records cannot.

04

Remember without freezing.

Interpretation can evolve while original evidence remains intact.

✳

The constraint changes the product: legacy private records that a hosting administrator can read do not qualify as owner-sealed records. The migration must strengthen that boundary instead of preserving it for compatibility.

    KEY CUSTODY / RECOVERY

    What survives a lost device?

    Owner-sealed encryption needs a recovery choice before existing histories are migrated. The node operator does not receive a universal recovery key.

    RECOVERY PATH AVAILABLE

    Restore through an enrolled device

    A separately enrolled, accessible device may authorize a replacement device to recover the protected history under an approved enrollment procedure.

    KEY AUTHORITYTHE OWNERNot the storage host. Local Iris access remains a separate, time-limited capability.
    Design scenarios only. Real recovery requires authenticated key enrollment, tested backups, a reversible migration and a successful device-loss drill.

    SOURCE & HONESTY NOTE

    A stronger design still has limits.

    This is a self-contained, synthetic visualization of a proposed protocol, not a live federation or a cryptographic audit. The browser-local encryption example actually runs on your device, but it does not implement a full key-management system. Graph layouts, ballots, times, scores, and device feasibility positions are explanatory, not empirical measurements.

    • Authorized recipients can still copy information that they receive in readable form.
    • Multi-hop paths do not promise perfect anonymity, particularly against traffic-correlation adversaries.
    • Key rotation protects future communication, not plaintext already copied.
    • Hosting administrators should not receive universal access to personal records; legacy exposure cannot be retroactively undone.
    • Protected federation requires independently audited implementations, revocation semantics, and security-conformance tests.
    • General private AI over arbitrary encrypted inputs is research-stage. Owner-controlled local inference is the practical default.

    Architectural grounding: distributed replication and consistency theory; versioned and bitemporal data design; privacy-preserving cryptographic primitives; authenticated group communication; security-conformance testing; human-in-the-loop evaluation and causal failure attribution.
